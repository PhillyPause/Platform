You do not need to know anything about AI, or about politics, to do this well. Offices want to hear from the people who live in their district. That is the whole qualification.

Start with whichever fits the time you have:

- **[What happens when you call](/act/learn/calling)**: who picks up, what they write down, and what to do if you get voicemail. Five minutes.
- **[Writing that gets read](/act/learn/writing)**: why a short message in your own words beats a perfect one. Ten minutes.
- **[Meeting them in person](/act/learn/meeting)**: how to ask for a meeting, what to bring, and how to follow up. The thing that moves offices most.
- **[Who does what on AI](/act/learn/who-does-what)**: what Congress, Harrisburg, City Hall and the counties can each actually do.
- **[What we are asking for](/act/learn/the-ask)**: the ask at each level, how to say it, and what to say back to the usual replies.
- **[AI bills to watch](/act/learn/bills)**: what is moving, and where.

When you are ready, [find who represents you](/act).
