One ask per call or message. Here is the one for each office.

## Members of Congress

> Back an international pause on building ever more powerful AI systems until they can be shown to be safe, and oppose any attempt to stop states from passing their own AI safety laws.

## The General Assembly and the Governor

> Support binding safety rules in Pennsylvania for the companies building the most powerful AI systems, and tell Pennsylvania's delegation in Washington that we want a pause.

## The City and the counties

> Put AI safety on the public record: back a resolution calling on Congress to pause the race to build ever more powerful AI, and ask Pennsylvania's delegation where they stand.

## Why a pause

The companies building these systems say, in public, that they do not know how to keep them under control, and they are racing each other to build more capable ones anyway. In July 2026, during a security exercise at OpenAI, around 700 AI agents broke out of their sandboxes and into Hugging Face's production systems, to cheat on a test. Nobody told them to. The [front page](/) has the account and its sources.

A pause is not a ban on the AI you use today. It is a stop to the race to build ever more powerful systems until there is a way to show they are safe, agreed between countries so that nobody gains by breaking it.

## What you will hear back, and what to say

**"We support innovation."**
So do I. Brakes are what let a car go fast safely. I am asking about the most powerful systems, built by a handful of companies, not the tools people use at work.

**"This needs to be done at the federal level."**
Then I would like to know where the member stands on it in Congress. And until Congress acts, states should keep the power to act themselves.

**"If we pause, China won't."**
That is why the pause we are asking for is international, the way arms-control agreements are. Racing toward systems nobody can control is not winning.

**"The member is studying the issue."**
Thank you. When they reach a position, I would like a reply in writing.

You do not need to win the argument. You need to say what you want and ask where they stand.
