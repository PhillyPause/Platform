What is on the table, kept short. Each item links to its source; if something here is out of date, [tell us](mailto:support@phillypause.org?subject=AI%20bills%20page).

## In Washington

**Stopping states from regulating AI.** In 2025 a ten-year moratorium on enforcing state AI laws was written into the federal budget bill. On July 1, 2025 the Senate voted 99 to 1 to take it out. The idea has kept coming back, including by executive order in December 2025. Ask your members of Congress where they stand on it.

**Evaluating the most powerful systems.** Several Senate bills would have the federal government test advanced AI systems for dangerous capabilities before they are released. None has passed.

## In other states

**California** passed the Transparency in Frontier Artificial Intelligence Act (SB 53) in September 2025: the largest AI developers must publish how they manage catastrophic risk, report serious safety incidents, and protect employees who raise the alarm.

**New York** passed the RAISE Act in December 2025, with similar requirements for the largest developers.

Both show that a state can act. Pennsylvania has not yet.

## In Harrisburg

**Deepfakes.** In 2025 Pennsylvania made it a crime to create and spread AI-generated forgeries of real people to defraud or injure them (Senate Bill 649).

Bills on AI in health-insurance decisions, on chatbots and children, and on disclosure of AI in political advertising have been introduced in the General Assembly. Nothing yet addresses the safety of the most powerful systems themselves, which is the gap a state law like California's fills.
