What is on the table, kept short, each with its source. If something here is out of date, [tell us](mailto:support@phillypause.org?subject=AI%20bills%20page).

## In Washington

**Whether states may make their own AI rules.** This is the fight that keeps coming back.

- A ten-year moratorium on enforcing state AI laws was written into the 2025 budget bill. On July 1, 2025 the Senate voted 99 to 1 to take it out ([roll call](https://www.senate.gov/legislative/LIS/roll_call_votes/vote1191/vote_119_1_00363.htm)).
- An attempt to add the same thing to the defence bill was dropped in December 2025 ([TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2025/12/03/another-bid-to-block-state-ai-regulation-has-failedfor-now)).
- Executive Order 14365, signed December 11, 2025, set up a Justice Department task force to challenge state AI laws ([Federal Register](https://www.federalregister.gov/documents/2025/12/16/2025-23092/ensuring-a-national-policy-framework-for-artificial-intelligence)). In March 2026 the White House asked Congress to override state laws outright ([Roll Call](https://rollcall.com/2026/03/20/white-house-ai-framework-calls-for-preemption-of-state-laws/)).

**The most powerful systems themselves.**

- The **Ban Artificial Superintelligence Act** (S. 5493), introduced September 23, 2026, would prohibit building it ([bill text](https://www.govinfo.gov/content/pkg/BILLS-119s5493is/html/BILLS-119s5493is.htm)). The closest thing in Congress to what we are asking for.
- The **Artificial Intelligence Risk Evaluation Act** (S. 2938) would have the government test advanced systems for dangerous capabilities before release ([bill text](https://www.govinfo.gov/content/pkg/BILLS-119s2938is/html/BILLS-119s2938is.htm)).
- The **AI LEAD Act** (S. 2937) would let people harmed by AI products sue the companies that make them ([sponsor](https://www.durbin.senate.gov/newsroom/press-releases/durbin-hawley-introduce-bill-allowing-victims-to-sue-ai-companies)).
- The **AI Kill Switch Act**, introduced in the House in July 2026, would require a way to shut systems down ([sponsor](https://lieu.house.gov/media-center/press-releases/reps-lieu-and-moran-introduce-bill-require-kill-switch-ai-systems-can)).

**Children and chatbots.** The **GUARD Act** (S. 3062), on age checks and safeguards for AI companions, passed the Senate Judiciary Committee unanimously in April 2026 ([bill text](https://www.govinfo.gov/content/pkg/BILLS-119s3062rs/html/BILLS-119s3062rs.htm)).

## In other states

**California** passed the Transparency in Frontier Artificial Intelligence Act (SB 53) in September 2025, and **New York** the RAISE Act in December 2025: the largest AI developers must publish how they manage catastrophic risk and report serious safety incidents. Both show a state can act on the systems themselves.

## In Harrisburg

**Already law.**

- **Digital forgery** (Act 35 of 2025): a crime to create and spread AI forgeries of real people to defraud or injure them ([SB 649](https://www.palegis.us/legislation/bills/2025/sb649)).
- **AI-generated abuse images** of children (Act 125 of 2024) ([Senate Republicans](https://www.pasenategop.com/news/legislation-to-combat-ai-generated-deepfake-images-of-minors-in-pa-set-for-enactment/)).
- **Deceptive AI advertising** (Act 99 of 2026) ([SB 806](https://www.palegis.us/legislation/bills/2025/sb806)).

**Passed one chamber.**

- The House adopted a **resolution opposing a federal moratorium on state AI laws**, 131 to 72, on September 30, 2026 ([HR 425](https://www.palegis.us/legislation/bills/2025/hr425)).
- **SAFECHAT Act**: disclosures and safeguards for children using AI chatbots, passed the Senate 49 to 1 ([SB 1090](https://www.palegis.us/legislation/bills/2025/sb1090)).
- **AI companionship apps**: safety rules, passed the House ([HB 2006](https://www.palegis.us/legislation/bills/2025/hb2006)).
- **AI in health care and insurance decisions**, passed the House ([HB 1925](https://www.palegis.us/legislation/bills/2025/hb1925)).
- **Deepfakes of candidates** in campaigns, passed the House 203 to 0 ([HB 811](https://www.palegis.us/legislation/bills/2025/hb811)).

**Proposed.** An **Artificial Intelligence Safety Measures Act** was circulated for co-sponsors in July 2026 ([memo](https://www.palegis.us/senate/co-sponsorship/memo?memoID=48995)). Nothing has yet been introduced that does for Pennsylvania what California's law does: rules for the most powerful systems themselves.

**Data centres.** More than a dozen bills, on energy costs, local zoning and outright moratoriums, among them a House bill giving municipalities time to consider proposals, passed 201 to 1 ([HB 2496](https://www.palegis.us/legislation/bills/2025/hb2496)). The Governor signed an executive order on data centres' costs to consumers in August 2026 ([press release](https://www.pa.gov/governor/newsroom/2026-press-releases/governor-shapiro-signs-executive-order-on-data-center-developmen)).

## At home

- **Philadelphia** City Council held a hearing on the city's own use of AI in October 2025 ([Route Fifty](https://www.route-fifty.com/artificial-intelligence/2025/10/heated-city-council-hearing-philly-tech-officials-failed-their-first-ai-test/408907/)), and the city published rules for its departments' use of AI in March 2026 ([Technical.ly](https://technical.ly/civics/philadelphia-municipal-ai-rules-missing-committee/)). In September 2026 the Mayor set up a data-centre task force ([City of Philadelphia](https://www.phila.gov/2026-09-21-mayor-parker-highlights-administration-priorities-as-philadelphia-city-council-returns-to-session/)).
- **Montgomery County** has an advisory council on AI for the public good ([Patch](https://patch.com/pennsylvania/norristown/montco-launches-ai-public-good-advisory-board)) and adopted guidelines for the county's own use in 2026 ([GovTech](https://www.govtech.com/artificial-intelligence/montgomery-county-pa-has-taken-a-number-of-steps-on-ai)).
- **Chester and Montgomery** counties' planning commissions have written a guide for townships weighing data-centre proposals ([Chester County Planning](https://www.chescoplanning.org/News/2026/0317-DataCenterEvent.cfm)).
