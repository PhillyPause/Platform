## This website

- **No tracking.** No analytics, no advertising, no third-party pixels, no
  fingerprinting, and no cookies set by us while you read.
- **No account needed** to read anything here.
- **Fonts** are served from Google Fonts, which means Google sees the request.
  We are looking at self-hosting them to remove even that.
- **The video** on the home page is YouTube's, in its privacy-enhanced
  player. Nothing is fetched from YouTube until you press play; from then on
  Google sees you as it would on YouTube itself.
- **One note in your browser** records that you have seen the video, so it
  does not open on you twice. It stays on your device and is never sent to us.
- **Server logs** record requests, as every web server does. They are kept
  only as long as needed to run the site and diagnose faults.

## Contacting your representatives

The [action centre](/act) needs to know a little about you to help, and
keeps none of it:

- **Your address**, if you type it to find who represents you, goes to the
  US Census Bureau's geocoder, which returns your districts. Only the
  district numbers come back to you, in the page's address. We do not keep
  the address or write it to our logs. You can pick your districts by hand
  instead.
- **Your name, where you live and why it matters to you**, if you ask for a
  draft message or call script, are used to write it and then forgotten.
  When the page says an AI model is helping, they go to
  [DeepInfra](https://deepinfra.com/privacy), which runs the model, to shape
  the draft.
- **The message itself** you send from your own email or the office's own
  form. It never passes through us.

That is the complete list for the site itself.

## If you email us

We keep your message so we can reply and so we remember the conversation. We do
not add you to a mailing list because you emailed us.

## If you write to join us

We hold the email address you wrote from, and nothing else. No name unless you
sign it, no phone number, no address, no record of which emails you opened.

What we do **not** do with it:

- Sell, rent or share it with anyone.
- Use it to track you across other websites.
- Add you to anything else, or pass you to another organisation.

Ask us and we will tell you exactly what we hold about you, or delete it. You
do not need to give a reason.
