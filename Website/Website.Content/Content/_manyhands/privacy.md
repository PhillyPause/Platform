## If you sign up on this site

Joining, or signing up for something, on this site keeps a record so we can
tell you what is on and know who is coming. It holds:

- **Your name and email**, and whether you want emails about new activities.
- **What you like doing**, from the boxes you ticked, so we only tell you
  about things that match.
- **What you signed up for**, whether you were on the waitlist, and whether
  you were checked in at the door.
- **The emails we sent you**: the subject, when, and whether it was
  delivered. Not whether you opened them; we cannot tell, and do not try.
- **Your Discord account**, only if you link it, so we can message you there
  instead of by email.

Signing in sets one cookie, `phillypause.session`, which keeps you signed in
for up to 30 days. It holds who you are and nothing else, and signing out
removes it. Sign-in is by a link we email you; there is no password to leak.

Mail is sent through [Resend](https://resend.com/legal/privacy-policy), which
sees the address and the message, as any mail service must. The records are
kept in a database at our host, DigitalOcean. The organizers can
see them; nobody else can, and none of it is sold, rented or shared.

**You can see and delete all of it yourself.** Sign in and open
[your page](/volunteer/me): it downloads everything we hold about you as a
file, and deletes you with one button. Deleting removes your name, email,
interests and Discord link at once. What stays is that *someone* came to an
event, so a count of twelve people stays twelve, without you attached.

## If we contact you about policy

We keep a list of the offices and people we talk to about AI policy: elected
officials, their staff, and organisations. For each we hold the work contact
details they publish or give us, notes on our meetings, and the emails we
exchange. Members of Congress come from the public
[congress-legislators](https://github.com/unitedstates/congress-legislators)
dataset. If you are on that list and want to know what we hold, or want to be
taken off it, write to us.
