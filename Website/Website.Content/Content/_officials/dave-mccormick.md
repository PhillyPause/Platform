McCormick, an Army veteran, former Treasury under secretary and former chief executive of the hedge fund Bridgewater Associates, defeated Sen. Bob Casey in 2024 and took office in January 2025. He has made AI, energy and data-centre investment in Pennsylvania a signature issue.
