PhillyPause is a group of Philadelphians who think the race to build
superintelligent AI should stop until it can be shown to be safe.

## What we are

**An independent Philadelphia organisation**, loosely affiliated with
[PauseAI Global](https://pauseai.info) — a Dutch foundation, Stichting PauseAI.

Being specific about this, because it is the sort of thing that gets fudged:
we are not a chapter, a branch or a franchise. Nobody delegates authority to
us and nobody speaks for us. We are not a local group of PauseAI US. Nothing on
this site is an official statement of PauseAI Global, and they are not
responsible for our wording.

What the affiliation means in practice is shared analysis and a shared ask.
Much of the explanatory content here is **adapted from PauseAI Global under
CC BY 4.0** — a licence that grants permission in advance, which is why we did
not have to ask and neither does anyone adapting us. Every adapted page says so
at the bottom, and the full list is on the [licence page](/license).

Being independent is mostly an advantage. We decide our own priorities, we
answer to the people who turn up, and when we are wrong it is our mistake to
fix.

## What we actually do

- Monthly organising meetings in Center City.
- Constituent contact with Pennsylvania's congressional delegation.
- Tabling and flyering around campuses and markets.
- Talks and panels with student groups and faculty.
- Local press.

That is the whole list. We are small and we would rather do five things
properly.

## What we will not do

Worth stating plainly:

- **No property damage, no harassment, no intimidation.** Not as tactics, not
  as jokes.
- **No overstating the science.** If we cannot source a claim, we do not make
  it. If we get one wrong, we correct it in public.
- **No pretending to be bigger than we are.** We are a volunteer group, not an
  institution.
- **No personal attacks on researchers.** The argument is about incentives and
  institutions, not about whether individual engineers are bad people. Many of
  them agree with us.

We have adopted PauseAI Global's code of conduct and safeguarding protocol
rather than writing our own — a small group publishing policy documents it
cannot maintain is worse off than one pointing at maintained originals.

- [Code of conduct](https://pauseai.info/code-of-conduct)
- [Safeguarding protocol](https://pauseai.info/safeguarding-protocol)
