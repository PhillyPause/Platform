The lowest-commitment useful thing is to come to one meeting and see what it
is. Most people who organise with us now did exactly that and decided
afterwards.

## Come to a meeting

**Monthly organising meeting, Center City, first Tuesday.** About ninety
minutes. We review the month, divide up congressional outreach, plan tabling
shifts, and argue about tactics.

You do not need to prepare anything, know anything technical, or speak. Sitting
in the corner listening is a completely normal first meeting.

Email [join@phillypause.org](mailto:join@phillypause.org?subject=Count%20Me%20In) and we will send you
the room and the time.

## Get the monthly email

One email a month: what happened in Philadelphia, what happened in Washington,
and the one thing worth doing this month. One-click unsubscribe, no
questions asked, and we never pass your address to anyone.

Our volunteer sign-up system is being stood up now — until it is live, email
us and we will add you by hand.

## Do something before you join anything

You do not have to be part of a group to be useful:

- [Call your representative](/act/congress) — fifteen minutes, with a script.
- Talk to one person you know about it.
- Write a letter to the *Inquirer*.

Plenty of people do only this, and it counts.

## What we will ask of you

Nothing automatic. No dues, no quota, no obligation to attend. We ask that you
follow [PauseAI's code of conduct](https://pauseai.info/code-of-conduct) at our
events, which amounts to: be decent, do not harass anyone, do not break the
law on our behalf.

## What you can expect from us

- We will not spam you, sell your address, or add you to anything you did not
  ask for.
- We will tell you what happened with the thing you helped with.
- If we get something wrong in public, we will correct it in public.
- You can leave at any time and we will not chase you.

## Not in Philadelphia?

We organise here and only here. If you are elsewhere,
[PauseAI Global](https://pauseai.info/join) keeps a list of groups and
communities worldwide — there may already be one near you, and if there is not,
starting one is more achievable than it sounds.

You can also [call your own representative](/act/congress) from anywhere in
the United States. That works regardless of whether a group exists near you.
