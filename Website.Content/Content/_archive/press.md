We are a small volunteer group and we are happy to talk to journalists.

## Contact

[info@phillypause.org](mailto:info@phillypause.org?subject=Press%20enquiry). Say what you are working on
and your deadline; we will put you with whoever is best placed, or tell you
quickly that we are not the right people.

## What we can speak to

- Why Philadelphians are organising on AI risk, and who turns up.
- Local organising: tabling, constituent contact, campus work.
- What Pennsylvania's congressional delegation has and has not said.
- The argument for a pause, and the strongest objections to it.

## What we will not do

- **Speak for PauseAI Global or any other organisation.** We are independent
  and loosely affiliated; they have their own press contacts and ours are not
  theirs.
- **Claim technical authority we do not have.** For frontier research
  questions we will point you at researchers rather than guess.
- **Estimate numbers we have not counted.** If we do not know how many people
  came, we will say so.

## Boilerplate

Quotable as-is:

> PhillyPause is an independent volunteer organisation in Philadelphia
> campaigning for binding international limits on the training of frontier
> artificial intelligence systems, until their safety can be demonstrated. It
> is loosely affiliated with PauseAI Global.

Please do not describe us as a chapter, branch or local group of any national
organisation. We are not one.

## The name

**PhillyPause**, one word, capital P twice. Not "Philly Pause", not
"PhillyPAUSE".

## Logo and assets

We are between identities: the previous mark was derived from another
organisation's logo and has been retired, and the replacement is not finished.
So there is no logo to send you yet.

If you need a visual, please use a photograph of the group rather than a
wordmark. Ask and we will find you one we have the rights to.

For PauseAI Global's own brand assets — which are theirs, not ours, and are
not covered by the licence on this site's content — see
[pauseai.info/press](https://pauseai.info/press).
