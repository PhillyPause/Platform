## Before anything is chosen: the endorsement problem

These are six living or historical public figures who have **not** endorsed
PhillyPause. Their words are quoted accurately and attributed, which is fair.
A grid of their faces on a campaign page is a different claim: visually it reads
as *these people are with us*. Altman and Musk in particular are running the
companies we are arguing against.

Three ways to handle it, in order of how well they protect us:

1. **No portraits.** Names and affiliations only, as now. Nothing to
   misread, nothing to license, nothing to maintain.
2. **Portraits with an explicit line** above the block — something like
   "None of the people quoted here is connected to PhillyPause" — which
   removes the implication and is arguably more striking than silence.
3. **Portraits with no caveat.** The risk is a complaint of implied
   endorsement, and the people best placed to make it have lawyers.

My recommendation is 2 if you want faces, 1 if you want this shipped on
Tuesday without a second thought.

## Licence rules that apply to every CC file below

- **Credit must appear** — author, licence name, and a link to the licence.
- **Cropping to a circle is a modification.** CC requires that changes be
  indicated. "Cropped" is enough.
- **CC BY-SA** files: the cropped version you publish must itself be released
  under CC BY-SA. That is fine for a photo in a page, but it is a real
  obligation, and it is why I would favour the CC BY and public domain options
  where the choice exists.
- **Public domain** files need no credit legally. Credit them anyway.
