CURRENT EDIT
The 1280 × 720 preview runs for 76 seconds at 24 fps (1,824 frames).
The one-second animated lead-in and the complete ten-second closing hold
remain: narration and captions follow the same revision 4 timing, and the
closing card is still from 66 to 76 seconds. The existing revision 4 SRT/VTT
files remain the current caption tracks for this edit.

Revision 5 corrects line attachments throughout the affected scenes. Puppet
hands, crowns and robot shoulder rivets expose attachment points from the
actual articulated transforms. Control cords connect the nearest maker hand
to the corresponding robot shoulder and develop slack during THE PROBLEM.
The job-lift lines begin at the visible headwear/hair. Message threads run
from the pointing palms to ten exposed pins in the rotated message board.
Franklin's string joins his raised palm to the kite's bridle. Lightning tips
meet the rod tips, and their glow follows the rod's exact conductor geometry.

Every jar now contains the complete robot, including the pointing arm and
feet throughout the sway cycle. The fitted puppet is positioned relative to
the jar's base, with its feet on the platter. The finial has a short neck
joining it to the dome. The glass dome and base geometry are unchanged.

The instruction cylinder is one continuous silhouette. Both end caps and
their internal arcs share the side endpoints. SHARE THIS VIDEO and SCAN TO
JOIN now use the same Caslon font size, scale, box height, text baseline and
horizontal padding measured from actual glyph ink. The wider phrase has a
correspondingly wider box. The QR, copyright and final-card wording remain.

CONNECTED PAPER CRUMPLING
The handoffs at playback 20.410 and 36.900 seconds now fold one connected
sheet into an angular paper wad, which exits downward or left respectively.
Their durations remain 0.88 and 0.78 seconds; the other transition varieties
retain their established directions and timing.

paper_crumple.py creates a shared mesh of 63 vertices and 96 triangular
facets. Its left/right and top/bottom folds gain shaded ridges and show the
paper reverse. All adjoining facets share vertices; none receives an
independent fragment trajectory. Thirteen whole-sheet poses per handoff
produce the stepped origami motion. Source-image and renderer hashes refresh
the cache when artwork or the folding code changes.

This is a cumulative source overlay for the existing accepted revision 2
production project in the PhillyPause_Production_Files archive. From that
project's parent folder, merge this archive's production/ and analysis/
directories into the matching directories. Retain the original fonts,
artwork, Python dependencies, source mix and other base-project files.

Render the current website approval preview from the project parent:

  python3 production/render_review.py --output output/PhillyPause_Web_720p.mp4

The revised PCM mix is included as production/audio/mix_revision4.wav because
audio timing has not changed since revision 4. To reproduce that mix and the
current captions from the original mix and retained caption baseline:

  python3 production/revision4_timing.py

That command always shifts the unmodified baseline by one second; it does
not repeatedly shift the current captions. Keep the included baseline under
analysis/revision4_backup/production/, or use --caption-source to relocate it.

TIMELINE AND EDITABLE SOURCE
Film.frame() receives final playback time. Scene methods and STARTS use the
original story clock; the frame method applies the one-second offset once.
Outgoing cards and snapshots now explicitly use their requested story pose
clock and restore the prior clock afterward. INTRO_SECONDS=1,
END_HOLD_START=66 and DURATION=76.

The authoritative geometry is in film.py and art.py; transition scheduling
is in transitions.py, and connected folding is in paper_crumple.py. The
working archive includes new/rebuilt assets, caches and focused verification
reports. Whole-sheet fold poses can be represented as ordinary PNG layers
with transform keyframes. Individual creases are generated by the Python
source; they are not a native editable After Effects mesh.
